## About Me

I'm Komal Jaiswal, a DevOps Specialist at OpsTree Global, working at the intersection of **Cloud Infrastructure, DevOps Engineering, AI Solutions and Technical Communication.**

My work involves understanding complex engineering challenges, exploring technology solutions, translating technical capabilities into business value, and creating resources that help engineering teams and decision-makers understand emerging technologies.

I'm particularly interested in how Generative AI, LLMs and agentic workflows can transform traditional infrastructure and software delivery.

### What I Work On

- **Cloud Architecture:** AWS, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), infrastructure modernisation and cloud migration.
- **DevOps & Platform Engineering:** CI/CD, Kubernetes, Docker, Jenkins and infrastructure automation.
- **DevSecOps:** Pipeline security, vulnerability management and secure software delivery.
- **AI & GenAI:** LLMs, AI-driven engineering, agentic workflows and AI-powered automation.
- **Observability & AIOps:** Monitoring, incident management, logging and intelligent root-cause analysis.
- **Technical Solutions & GTM:** PRDs, solution documentation, technical proposals, whitepapers and enterprise case studies.

### Beyond Engineering

- DevOps and Generative AI trainer — 150+ students trained.
- Community speaker and technical knowledge-sharing enthusiast.
- Technical writer covering cloud, DevOps, AI and emerging technologies.
- Contributor to enterprise technical publications and solution narratives.
